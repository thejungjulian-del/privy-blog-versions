Guidelines

The rules for all five categories. Market and Announcement are on this page. The other three are already components in the team's Figma file.

Every cover

  1. One idea per cover. A logo lockup, a topic, or a product shot. Nothing else on top.
  2. Only real things. Partner logos from their press kits, real product screenshots, no made-up numbers, no AI images.
  3. Type. Titles in Geist Regular, small labels in Inter. White on gradients, ink #010110 on light backgrounds.
  4. Color. Violet #584FFF, coral #FF4D3C, purple #9A61FF, pink #FC6CEA and their tints. Grain on every gradient, about 30%.
  5. Logos. privy on the left, the partner on the right, a × between them, same visual height. Never stretch a partner logo or give it a color other than white or ink.
  6. Neighbors. Change the color from the cover next to it in the grid.
  7. Safe area. Keep logos and titles in the middle. Link previews on X and LinkedIn cut off the top and bottom.

Open questions for the leads

Things to settle before we hand this to Privy.

Size

Our covers are 16:10, but Privy's blog images are 1100 × 800. Do we switch, or does Privy crop?

Category names

The blog says "Announcement" and "Inside the stack". Our covers say "Announcements" and "Inside the Stack". Which one?

Launches

Privy files its own launches under Product and Engineering. Do they get the Announcement cover anyway?

Logos

Do we need each partner's OK to use their logo on a cover?

How it's made

  • Each cover is one SVG drawn in your browser. Nothing on this page is an image file, and nothing is AI-generated.
  • The look comes from the team's 11/18 Figma slides: blurred color, grain, white tiles with circles cut out, and the privy × partner lockup.
  • Logos: Ripple and Stripe from Simple Icons, TRON and Ramp from their own sites. Swap in the official press-kit files before anything ships.
  • Icons from Lucide. Font: Geist by Vercel (SIL Open Font License).
  • The same covers are Figma components with editable fields, on the "Round 4" page of the covers file.
