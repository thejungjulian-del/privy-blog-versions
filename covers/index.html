Recommendation

Keep the lilac. Give each post type its own layout.

Privy's recent covers almost all sit on the same lilac background with no title, so a launch, a partner post and an essay look alike in the gallery. The most consistent blogs here (Plaid, Vercel, Supabase, Circle) use one template with a few versions.

  • Launches: a product shot, like the Treasury Management cover
  • Customer stories: the two logos plus one big stat
  • Guides: the title, set big
  • Every cover: exported at 1200 × 630 so X and LinkedIn don't crop it

How this was madeOn Nov 12, 2026, each blog's index page was opened and the share image (the og:image tag) was saved from its newest posts. Coinbase blocked the request, and Paradigm and Base didn't list posts in a readable way. Dynamic's blog now redirects to Fireblocks, so Fireblocks is here instead. Figma is left out because its posts mostly share one default image. Each cover is sorted into the one approach it uses most. Click a cover for the full image and a link to the original post.
